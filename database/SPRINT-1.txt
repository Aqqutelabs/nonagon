Sprint 1: Equipment Register Foundation
Scope: four-sprint Equipment Management breakdown. Marketplace commercial
listings and transactions are a separate domain and are not published here.

Delivered foundation
- General navigation, equipment route, responsive three-column register.
- Owner-linked equipment, unique (owner_id, asset_code), statuses and taxonomy.
- Create and edit forms, company-private inline category creation, CSV import.
- Removal uses an explicit archive action (soft deletion) with exact AID entry.
  Open maintenance and active alerts block archival. Assignments are closed.
  Photos, financial observations, maintenance and audit history remain stored.
  Archived assets disappear from operational views, photos and KPI totals.
  AIDs remain reserved, and no restoration UI is included in this sprint.
- KPI cards, default card grid, sortable list, pagination and URL state.
- Loading, empty and recoverable register error states.
- Tenant/unit RBAC, assignment scope for operators, CSRF and mutation audits.
- Existing later-sprint search/detail features remain available.
