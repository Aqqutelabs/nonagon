Marketplace Sprint 1

Delivered
- Public marketplace landing, search, commercial/equipment/location/compliance/pricing filters, equipment cards, owner profiles, and public equipment detail pages.
- Owner workspace for existing Equipment assets and standard assets registered through Marketplace.
- Equipment registered through Marketplace is created as a standard Operational company asset, appears immediately in the main Equipment register, and remains there when a listing is paused or closed.
- Marketplace image uploads are linked to the same asset and can serve as its primary Equipment photo.
- Draft, preview, publish, pause, close, and edit listing lifecycle with permission checks and audit events.
- Lease and sale configuration, public pricing, availability periods, approved media, OEM/model suggestions, and marketplace-safe public projections.
- Searchable category, OEM, and OEM model controls. New OEM/model suggestions retain PENDING verification status.
- Structured lease terms: Hour/Day/Week/Month rate, minimum duration, deposit, operator rate, consumables, mobilization, demobilization, maintenance, and insurance.
- Structured sale terms: asking price, payment terms, inspection requirement, condition, taxes/fees, and additional notes.
- Canonical global country directory plus hierarchical region/city records. Listing rules store canonical location IDs as ALLOW or RESTRICT chips; no rules means unrestricted.
- Existing legacy lease/sale columns remain nullable and readable. Migration 012 backfills the unified lease rate without deleting legacy data.
